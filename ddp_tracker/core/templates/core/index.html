The DDP Tracker provides an overview of what data takeouts/data download packages (DDPs) of digital platforms contain, what their data points mean, how they compare across platforms, and how they change over time. To do so, people can upload DDPs and the DDP Tracker collects the structure of these takeouts into a collective knowledge base.

Good to know: Uploaded DDPs are parsed and deleted right away: only their structure is kept, file names are stored anonymized, and the values found in your file are visible to you alone unless you decide to share them with the community.

Explore

  • See what a platform's DDP contains

    Every data point of its takeouts as a tree, with its type, examples and meaning. Filter by request date and account language.

  • Platform annotations: Look up what a data point means

    Each data point with its description, where it is found and in which languages.

  • Representations: Compare meanings across platforms

    Shared concepts, like a watched video, linked to the data points that hold them on each platform. The vocabulary lists the terms they use.

  • Documentation: Learn how the DDP Tracker works

    What "new", "changed" and "missing" mean, how uploads are checked, how your privacy is protected, etc.
