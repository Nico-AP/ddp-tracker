How it works

  1. Upload

    Someone requests their DDP from a platform and uploads it.

  2. Parse

    The tracker notes the structure (which fields exist, of what type) and deletes the file.

  3. Annotate

    Curators describe what each data point means, in terms shared across platforms.

  4. Explore

    Everyone can explore the result, platform by platform.
